One tiny thing first.

You need the Don't Start Without Me extension in Chrome. Two minutes, tops.

  1. Download it: extension ZIP (or ask your host to send it), then unzip.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the extension folder.
  4. Come back here and reload this page.
